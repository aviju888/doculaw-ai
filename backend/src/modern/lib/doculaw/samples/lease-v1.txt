RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement (this "Lease") is entered into on August 14, 2026, by and between Bramblecote Properties LLC, a California limited liability company ("Landlord"), and Marlowe Quintero ("Tenant"). Landlord and Tenant are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

1. PREMISES. Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, the residential dwelling unit located at 2217 Tamsin Court, Unit 4, Fernhollow, California 90000 (the "Premises"), together with one (1) assigned parking space (Space No. 12) and the non-exclusive use of the shared laundry room.

2. TERM. The term of this Lease shall commence on September 1, 2026 and shall terminate at 11:59 p.m. on August 31, 2027 (the "Initial Term"), unless sooner terminated as provided herein.

3. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL. Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Lease shall automatically renew for successive terms of twelve (12) months each (each, a "Renewal Term") upon the same terms and conditions, unless either Party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other Party not less than thirty (30) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

4. RENT.

4.1 Monthly Rent. Tenant shall pay to Landlord monthly rent in the amount of One Thousand Eight Hundred Fifty Dollars ($1,850.00) ("Rent"), payable in advance on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month, without demand, deduction, or offset.

4.2 Method of Payment. Rent shall be paid through Landlord's online resident portal or by personal check delivered to Landlord's office. A processing fee of Three Dollars and Ninety-Five Cents ($3.95) shall apply to each payment made by credit or debit card.

4.3 Rent Adjustment. Landlord may increase the Rent effective as of the first day of any Renewal Term by delivering written notice to Tenant not less than thirty (30) days in advance. No single increase shall exceed eight percent (8%) of the Rent then in effect.

5. LATE CHARGES. If any installment of Rent is not received by Landlord by 11:59 p.m. on the fifth (5th) day of the month in which it is due:

(a) Tenant shall pay a late charge of Seventy-Five Dollars ($75.00); and

(b) an additional late charge of Ten Dollars ($10.00) per day shall accrue for each subsequent day that such Rent remains unpaid, not to exceed a total of Two Hundred Fifty Dollars ($250.00) in any calendar month.

6. RETURNED PAYMENTS. Tenant shall pay a fee of Thirty-Five Dollars ($35.00) for each check or electronic payment that is returned unpaid for any reason. After two (2) returned payments, Landlord may require that all future payments be made by cashier's check or money order.

7. SECURITY DEPOSIT.

(a) Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of Two Thousand Seven Hundred Seventy-Five Dollars ($2,775.00) as security for the faithful performance of Tenant's obligations (the "Security Deposit").

(b) Landlord may apply all or any portion of the Security Deposit toward:

(i) unpaid Rent and other sums due under this Lease;

(ii) the cost of repairing damage to the Premises beyond ordinary wear and tear; and

(iii) the cost of cleaning the Premises upon move-out, including professional carpet cleaning, which Tenant agrees shall be charged in all cases regardless of the condition of the carpet.

(c) Landlord shall return the Security Deposit, less any lawful deductions, together with an itemized statement of deductions, within twenty-one (21) days after Tenant vacates the Premises and returns all keys.

(d) In addition to the Security Deposit, Tenant shall pay a non-refundable administrative fee of Two Hundred Fifty Dollars ($250.00) upon execution of this Lease.

8. UTILITIES. Tenant shall be responsible for arranging and paying for all electricity, gas, internet, and cable services. Tenant shall also pay a flat monthly utility fee of Sixty-Five Dollars ($65.00) for water, sewer, and trash collection, payable together with Rent.

9. USE AND OCCUPANCY. The Premises shall be used solely as a private residence for Tenant and the following additional occupants: none. Any guest staying more than fourteen (14) days in any six (6) month period shall require Landlord's prior written consent.

10. PETS. No animal, bird, reptile, fish tank, or other pet of any kind shall be kept on or about the Premises, even temporarily, without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord may withhold in its sole discretion. Service animals and assistance animals are not pets for purposes of this Section.

11. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not assign this Lease, sublet all or any portion of the Premises, or advertise the Premises on any short-term rental platform. Any attempted assignment or sublease without Landlord's prior written consent shall be void and shall constitute a material default under this Lease.

12. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS.

12.1 Tenant's Obligations. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and sanitary, promptly notify Landlord in writing of any leak, mold, or malfunction, and replace smoke detector batteries and light bulbs as needed. Tenant shall be responsible for the cost of any repair made necessary by the negligence or misuse of Tenant or Tenant's guests.

12.2 Landlord's Obligations. Landlord shall maintain the roof, plumbing, heating, and electrical systems in good working order and shall commence repairs within seven (7) days after receipt of written notice from Tenant, or within twenty-four (24) hours for conditions that materially threaten health or safety.

13. ENTRY BY LANDLORD.

(a) Landlord and its agents may enter the Premises at reasonable times to inspect, make necessary repairs, or show the Premises to prospective tenants, purchasers, or lenders, upon not less than twenty-four (24) hours' written notice to Tenant.

(b) Landlord may enter the Premises at any time without notice in the event of an emergency or if Landlord reasonably believes that Tenant has abandoned the Premises.

(c) During the final thirty (30) days of the term, Landlord may place customary "For Rent" signs on the Premises and may show the Premises upon two (2) hours' notice.

14. EARLY TERMINATION. Tenant may terminate this Lease before the expiration of the then-current term only if Tenant:

(a) delivers not less than sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Landlord; and

(b) pays to Landlord an early termination fee equal to two (2) months' Rent, in addition to all Rent due through the termination date.

15. HOLDOVER. If Tenant remains in possession of the Premises after the expiration or termination of this Lease without Landlord's written consent, Tenant shall pay holdover rent of One Hundred Twenty-Five Dollars ($125.00) per day and shall be liable for all damages caused by the holdover.

16. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY.

16.1 Renter's Insurance. Tenant shall maintain, at Tenant's expense, a renter's insurance policy with personal liability coverage of not less than One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000.00) and shall name Landlord as an additional insured.

16.2 Indemnification. Tenant shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Landlord and its members, managers, agents, and employees from and against any and all claims, losses, damages, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or related to the use of the Premises by Tenant or Tenant's guests, except to the extent caused by Landlord's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

16.3 Limitation of Liability. Landlord shall not be liable to Tenant or Tenant's guests for any loss of or damage to personal property, or for any bodily injury, resulting from theft, fire, water, mold, or the acts or omissions of other residents, regardless of the cause.

17. DISPUTE RESOLUTION.

(a) Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Lease shall be resolved exclusively by final and binding arbitration before a single arbitrator selected by Landlord, and not by a court of law.

(b) Tenant agrees that any claim shall be brought solely in Tenant's individual capacity and not as a plaintiff or class member in any purported class, collective, or representative proceeding.

(c) EACH PARTY KNOWINGLY AND VOLUNTARILY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY.

18. ATTORNEYS' FEES. In any action or proceeding to enforce or interpret this Lease, the prevailing Party shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

19. RULES AND REGULATIONS. Tenant shall comply with the Community Rules attached as Addendum A. Landlord may amend the Community Rules at any time by posting notice in the common areas or delivering notice to Tenant, and any such amendment shall be binding on Tenant as if fully set forth in this Lease.

20. GENERAL PROVISIONS.

20.1 Notices. All notices under this Lease shall be in writing and delivered personally, by first-class mail, or by email to the addresses listed below the Parties' signatures.

20.2 Governing Law and Venue. This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of California. Any action not subject to arbitration shall be brought exclusively in the state courts located in Marisco County, California.

20.3 Entire Agreement. This Lease, including Addendum A, is the entire agreement between the Parties and may be modified only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. If any provision of this Lease is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Lease as of the date first written above.

LANDLORD: Bramblecote Properties LLC

ADDENDUM A: COMMUNITY RULES

1. Quiet hours are from 10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. daily.

2. Grills, smokers, and open flames are prohibited on balconies and patios.

3. Bicycles shall be stored only in the designated bike room, and any bicycle left in a common area may be removed at Tenant's expense.
